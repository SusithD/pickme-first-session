# First-session teardown: how to capture it

Goal: a screen-by-screen record of what a brand-new rider sees from install to a first booking.

## Setup
- Use a phone that has never had PickMe installed, or uninstall it and clear its data first.
- Use a phone number that has never had a PickMe account, if possible (a family member's, with their permission). Otherwise, note that the account already existed.
- Turn on screen recording before you open the Play Store (Android) or App Store (iOS).
- Record on Android if you can: most Sri Lankan riders use it.

## Capture
Record one continuous video. Narrate out loud what you expect and what surprises you.

1. Store listing: app size, rating, first screenshots.
2. First launch: splash, language choice, intro slides.
3. Signup: phone number, OTP, name, email, terms. Note how long the OTP takes.
4. Permissions: location and notifications. When are they asked, and is a reason given?
5. Home screen: the first thing you see, and how many services are offered.
6. Booking a ride: set pickup and drop, vehicle options, fare display, ETA, payment method, promo codes.
7. Stop before confirming. Do not book a real ride unless you actually need one.
8. If you did take a real trip, also capture the in-ride screens, payment, and the rating step.

Then also capture the first screens of PickMe Food, to see how the app introduces a second service.

## After
- Export screenshots of every distinct screen into `research/teardown/` and name them in order: `01-store.png`, `02-splash.png`, and so on.
- Note anything that confused you, slowed you down, or almost made you quit, with the timestamp.
- Repeat the signup in Sinhala or Tamil if the app offers it, and note copy that breaks or reads poorly.

## 2. Services in the app

- **Rides (vehicle types):** Bike, Tuk, Flex (small hatchback), Mini (large hatchback), Car (sedan), Minivan, Luxury/VIP. Listing also mentions Nano, Sedan, Van [S4, S7]. Vehicle names differ by source; treat as indicative.
- **Ride features:** live driver tracking, fare estimate, driver name/photo/plate shown after match, ride scheduling, day booking [S7].
- **PickMe Food:** food delivery, restaurants in Colombo and major towns [S4, S7].
- **PickMe Trucks:** logistics, Light and Mover categories, GPS tracking [S8].
- **PickMe Flash:** parcel send/receive (named on a Similarweb listing) [S7] - unverified.
- **PickMe Market:** NOT FOUND in any search result. Treat as unverified/not confirmed.
- **Other:** goods purchasing, parcel delivery, tour packages [S7].

## 4. Payments, languages, signup

**Payments (history; current menu NOT verified):**
- Cash: original default; company said lack of card support was a difference from Uber (2015-era coverage) [S12].
- Visa/Mastercard in-app payment: HNB gateway, 2017 [S13].
- American Express: via Nations Trust Bank, 2020 [S14].
- QR payments: 2018 (iPay, UPay); Dialog/Genie QR, 2019 [S15, S16].
- Reported 2026 lists (FriMi, PayHere, bank transfer, cash on delivery) from low-reliability sites. Unverified.
- PickMe Credit: a Peoples Bank product exists (PDF, Mar 2025) [S17]. Relationship to in-app wallet unverified.
- "PickMe Pay" / in-app wallet: NOT CONFIRMED in results.

**Languages:** English, Sinhala, Tamil. Sinhala and Tamil added at trilingual launch, Dec 2015 [S12, S18]. App Store lists Singhalese and Tamil [S7].

**Signup method:** NOT FOUND. Unverified (likely phone number/OTP; not checked).

## 5. Competitors

- **Uber:** main rival. Launch date conflicting: Dec 2015 (Echelon, undated year inferred; Daily Mirror "official launch in Colombo"). Galle trips 9 Dec 2017 [S19, S20]. Rest of World (Mar 2024): Uber operates in five districts and connects 40 cities; declined to disclose Sri Lanka fleet size [S4].
- **Market share:** No formal data found. An older unsourced claim of 80-90% share of taxi aggregator market (undated). Unverified. HNB analysis says share data unavailable but PickMe "widely perceived" to lead [S11].
- **Kangaroo Cabs, Yogo:** NOT FOUND in any result. Unverified; not researched further.
- **Driver-side context:** Driver commission 12% per ride for tuk-tuk drivers, 7% above Rs 8,000/day [S4]. Uber fare increases raised driver complaints [S20].

## 6. Growth, retention, acquisition, promotions

- **Growth statements (company/press):** drivers added consistently over 5 years; pandemic growth via essential goods and hospital-staff transport; FY25/26 platform activity +47% YoY for 9 months; average monthly driver earnings +14% [S4, S10].
- **Retention:** No retention rate published. Company cites driver perks (insurance, incentives, school supplies, discounts for top-rated drivers) as differentiators. Some drivers report leaving due to harassment and falling earnings [S4].
- **Acquisition:** No customer acquisition cost or channel data. Only the FY24/25 new-consumer peak (>250,000 in one month) [S9].
- **First-ride promo for new users:** NOT FOUND. Only expired bank-card offers: Nations Trust Amex 30% off, max LKR 500/transaction, ended 24 May 2026 [S21]; Peoples Bank PBR24 20% off, ended 31 May 2024 [S22]; Peoples Bank tuk offer ended 30 Apr 2025 [S22]. App help text says promo codes are entered at checkout [S23].

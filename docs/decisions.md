## Open points found while consolidating the plan

These are not new decisions. Each needs an owner's call before the affected work starts.

1. **H1 assignment before signup.** Plan section 5 assigns riders at first fare view and keeps them sticky by account. Under the H5 treatment the fare is shown before any account exists, so H1 assignment must be keyed to an install ID and carried onto the account at signup. See `docs/experiment-plan.md`.
2. **Demo toggle versus separate live tests.** The section 10 work breakdown makes `partAVariant` follow the single global variant toggle. That is acceptable for the demo. Live tests must not share one toggle, because H1 and H5 have to stay attributable (D15 and section 10).
3. **Duration claim.** Plan section 5 says the sample arrives "within days" at a tenth of the peak month. The arithmetic does not support that (about 27 days of fare views, before any geography or new-rider filter). Corrected in `docs/experiment-plan.md`.
4. **Primary metric denominator.** S8 is defined on first completed rides, which the treatment can change. A fare-view-based sensitivity check is proposed in `docs/experiment-plan.md`; it is not yet approved.
5. **Translation scope count.** D7 says five screens; section 4 lists six. Section 4 is used.
6. **Crash-free and support thresholds.** Plan section 5 gives no size for "crash-free sessions unchanged" or "support contacts per 100 trips up". Thresholds must be fixed before launch.
